# Privacy Policy — SmartBudgetSite Backups

Effective date: November 24, 2026

## Purpose

SmartBudgetSite Backups is a private operational integration used exclusively by the SmartBudgetSite administrator for off-site backup and recovery.

## Google Drive access

The application uses rclone and the Google Drive API with the limited `drive.file` OAuth scope.

Google Drive access is used solely to create, retrieve, manage, and delete authorized backup files.

## Data protection

Backup contents and filenames are encrypted locally using rclone crypt before transmission to Google Drive.

Encryption credentials are managed separately from Google OAuth credentials.

Google user data is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or AI model training and is not sold or shared for unrelated purposes.

## Data retention and deletion

Backup files are retained according to operational backup and recovery requirements.

The administrator can delete backup files and revoke the application's Google Account access.
